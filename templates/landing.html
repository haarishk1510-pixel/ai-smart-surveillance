A camera that checks who is there and responds on its own.

It verifies faces from a live feed, then captures evidence, emails an alert, logs the event and sounds a hardware alarm when someone isn't recognized.

How it works

From a camera frame to an alarm

  1. Capture

    OpenCV reads frames from the camera.

  2. Verify

    DeepFace (FaceNet) compares every 10th frame with enrolled faces.

  3. Confirm

    Several checks must agree, so one bad frame doesn't trigger an alarm.

  4. Respond

    A background thread saves evidence, uploads it to S3 and sends an SNS email.

  5. Signal

    Events reach MySQL and the dashboard. The Arduino drives an LED and buzzer.
